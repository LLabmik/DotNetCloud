# Chat — User Guide

> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-27

---

## Welcome

DotNetCloud Chat is a real-time messaging platform with channels, direct messages, file sharing, and audio/video calls. Use it to keep team conversations organized and stay connected with the people you work with.

---

## Channels

Channels are shared conversation spaces for a topic, project, or team.

### Channel Types

| Type                | Description                                                               |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Public channel**  | Visible to everyone in the organization — great for team-wide discussions |
| **Private channel** | Invite-only, for a specific group of people                               |

### Creating a Channel

1. Open **Chat** from the left sidebar
2. Click the **+** button in the Chat sidebar
3. Enter a name
4. Choose a type — **Public** or **Private**
5. Click **Create**

### Channel Actions

From the channel header you can:

- **Pin** — pin the channel to the top of the sidebar (drag pinned channels to reorder them)
- **Mute** — silence its notifications
- **Invite** — add a user to the channel
- **Add People** — add multiple members
- **Edit** — change the name and topic
- **Archive** — archive the channel
- **Leave** — leave the channel

Owners and admins can manage members from the member list. Use the search box in the sidebar to find channels.

---

## Direct Messages

Direct messages (DMs) are private conversations between you and one or more people.

### Starting a DM

1. Click **New DM** in the sidebar
2. Select the person (or people, for a group DM) to message
3. The conversation opens in the message view

Direct messages are private between participants. You can **block** a user — a blocked user cannot send you messages or call you.

---

## Messaging

### Sending a Message

1. Type your message in the input bar at the bottom
2. Press **Enter** to send, or **Shift+Enter** for a new line
3. Use the formatting toolbar for bold, italic, code blocks, and quotes

### Mentions

Type **@** to mention someone, with autocomplete. Mentions notify the mentioned user, and you can also mention a whole channel or **@all**.

### Attachments

- **Attach files** from your computer using the attachment button
- **Paste an image** from your clipboard to send it directly
- Attached files are integrated with the Files module

### Message Actions

Hover over a message to:

- **Reply** — start a threaded reply
- **Edit** — correct a message you sent
- **Delete** — remove a message you sent
- **React** — add an emoji reaction

### Searching Messages

Use the search icon in the channel header to find messages within the current channel.

## Audio & Video Calls

Start an audio or video call from any channel or direct message.

### Starting a Call

1. Open a channel or DM
2. Click the **phone** (audio) or **camera** (video) button in the header
3. In a call you can **mute**, turn your **camera** on or off, **share your screen**, and **invite** more people
4. If a call is already active, use **Join Call** instead

The **Calls** button shows call history with a call-back option. See the [Video & Audio Calls](VIDEO_CALLS.md) guide for full details.

## Announcements & Notifications

### Announcements

Administrators can post announcements that appear as a banner at the top of the chat, with a priority level and acknowledgement tracking.

### Notification Preferences

Open your notification preferences to:

- Enable **push notifications** to your devices
- Configure **per-channel mute** settings
- Choose what you're notified about (all messages, mentions only, or none)

## Presence

Your presence status tells others whether you're available:

| Status             | Meaning                                                 |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Online**         | You're active                                           |
| **Away**           | You're idle                                             |
| **Do Not Disturb** | You don't want interruptions (calls still come through) |
| **Offline**        | You're not connected                                    |

You can set a custom status message alongside your presence.

---

## Tips & Tricks

- Use **channels** for topic-specific discussions and **DMs** for private conversations.
- **Pin** frequently used channels to the top of the sidebar for quick access.
- **Mute** noisy channels and use search to find messages within a channel.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                      | What to Do                                                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Messages not sending       | Check your internet connection and that the Chat module is running                              |
| Notifications not arriving | Check your per-channel mute settings and notification preferences                               |
| Can't join a call          | Ensure your browser has camera/microphone permission; see [Video & Audio Calls](VIDEO_CALLS.md) |
| Channel missing            | You may have left it or lost access — ask a channel owner to re-invite you                      |
