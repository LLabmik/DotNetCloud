# Video — User Guide

> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-27

---

## Welcome

DotNetCloud Video lets you browse, play, and manage your video library directly from your browser. Stream your personal video collection anywhere, with series support, subtitles, and transcoding.

---

## Browsing Your Library

Use the sidebar to switch between:

- **Home** — recently added videos
- **Library** — series first, then standalone videos
- **Series** — browse by series
- **Favorites** — videos you've starred
- **Library Settings** — configure and scan your library

Use the **search bar** to find specific titles. Cards show thumbnails, title, duration, file size, and resolution.

---

## Series

Series are detected automatically when scanning your library. Files named like `Show.Name.S01E01.mp4` or organized in `Show Name/Season 01/` folders are grouped together. Open a series to browse its seasons and episodes.

---

## Playback

Click a video to start playback in the built-in player, which supports:

- Play/pause
- Seeking
- Volume control
- Fullscreen
- Playback speed adjustment

### Supported Formats

MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM, and FLV. The player shows codec and container information and can display available **subtitles**. Content is delivered directly, remuxed, or transcoded as needed. Watch progress and history are tracked.

### Favorites

Use the star button in the player to favorite a video and find it again in the **Favorites** section.

### Editing Metadata

Use **Edit metadata** from the player to correct a video's title or other details.

---

## Library Settings

Open **Library Settings** to configure and scan your video library.

> **Note:** Resetting the collection removes indexed metadata (videos, collections, subtitles, watch progress, watch history, shares) — your actual video files are not affected.

---

## Tips & Tricks

- Name series files consistently (`Show.Name.S01E01.mp4`) so they group into series automatically.
- Adjust playback speed for educational or lecture content.
- Search across your entire library to find videos instantly.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue               | What to Do                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Video won't play    | The format may require transcoding — allow a moment for it to start; check your connection |
| Series not grouped  | Rename files consistently (`Show.Name.S01E01.mp4`) and rescan the library                  |
| Subtitles missing   | Subtitles must be available alongside the video; verify the file                           |
| Watch progress lost | Resetting the collection clears watch progress and history                                 |
