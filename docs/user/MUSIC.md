# Music — User Guide

> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-27

---

## Welcome

DotNetCloud Music lets you browse, play, and manage your music library directly from your browser. Stream your personal music collection anywhere.

Use the sidebar to switch between **Library**, **Artists**, **Albums**, **Genres**, **Playlists**, **Favorites**, **Recently Played**, and **Library Settings**.

---

## Browsing Your Library

- Browse by **Artists**, **Albums**, or **Genres** using the sidebar navigation
- The **Library** home shows recommendations based on your listening history
- **Recently Played** lists your playback history
- Use the **search bar** in the toolbar to find specific songs, albums, or artists

---

## Playback

- Click a song (or double-click a track row) to play it
- The player at the bottom provides **play/pause**, **previous/next track**, **volume**, and a **progress bar**

### Shuffle & Repeat

- Toggle **shuffle** for random playback
- Cycle **repeat** between **All**, **One**, and **Off**

### Queue

Use the **⋯** menu on any track to **Add to Queue**.

### Visualizer

While music is playing, click **Visualizer** in the toolbar to open a full visualizer. Choose a preset (or load all presets), auto-cycle presets, or go fullscreen. Requires a browser with WebGL2 support.

---

## Playlists

### Creating a Playlist

1. Click **+ New Playlist**
2. Give it a name and optional description
3. Mark it **Public** to share it with other users

### Editing a Playlist

- Open a playlist to play it
- Add tracks with the **⋯** menu (**Add to playlist**)
- Remove tracks, edit, or delete the playlist

Playlists shared by other users appear under **Public Playlists**.

---

## Favorites

Star (★) tracks and albums to add them to the **Favorites** section for quick access.

---

## Library Settings

Open **Library Settings** to manage your music:

- **Sources** — one or more Files folders scanned into the library
- **Scan Now / Cancel Scan** — scan with live progress
- **Metadata Enrichment** — automatically fetch metadata and cover art from MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, and TheAudioDB, or manually fetch artist logos, all cover art, or all metadata
- **Reset collection** — removes indexed data only; your audio files are not affected

---

## Tips & Tricks

- Use **playlists** to create mood-based or genre-specific listening sessions.
- **Favorites** are a quick way to access your most-played tracks.
- Search across your entire library to find songs instantly.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                 | What to Do                                                                           |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Tracks not appearing  | Run **Scan Now** from Library Settings and confirm your source folders are correct   |
| Playback won't start  | Check that your browser supports the audio format and that your connection is stable |
| Cover art missing     | Run metadata enrichment (MusicBrainz / Cover Art Archive) from Library Settings      |
| Visualizer won't open | The visualizer requires a browser with WebGL2 support                                |
