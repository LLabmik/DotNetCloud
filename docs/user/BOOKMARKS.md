# Bookmarks — User Guide

> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-27

---

## Welcome

DotNetCloud Bookmarks lets you save, organize, and search web links. Keep your favorite pages organized in folders with tags for easy retrieval — and sync them with your browser using the [browser extension](BROWSER_EXTENSION.md).

---

## Browsing Bookmarks

- Browse all bookmarks from the **All Bookmarks** view
- Star favorites for quick access
- Switch between **Grid** and **List** view with the toolbar toggle

---

## Adding Bookmarks

1. Click **+ New**
2. Enter the **URL**
3. Optionally add:
   - **Title**
   - **Description**
   - **Notes**
   - **Folder**
   - **Tags** (comma-separated)

### Preview

Open a bookmark to see its saved details. Click **Fetch Preview** to fetch or refresh the page's resolved title, description, favicon, site name, and preview image.

### Saving from Your Browser

Use the DotNetCloud [browser extension](BROWSER_EXTENSION.md) to save bookmarks directly while you browse.

---

## Organizing Bookmarks

### Folders

Create folders in the sidebar to group bookmarks by category (e.g., "Work", "Reading", "Reference").

### Tags

Add tags to bookmarks for cross-folder categorization. Filter by tag to find related bookmarks quickly.

### Favorites

Star important bookmarks for quick access.

---

## Searching Bookmarks

Type in the toolbar search box and click the search button to find bookmarks by title, URL, or tags.

---

## Importing & Exporting

- **Import** — import bookmarks from standard browser HTML export files (`bookmarks.html`)
- **Export** — export your bookmarks as HTML for backup or migration

---

## Tips & Tricks

- Use **folders** for broad categories and **tags** for cross-cutting topics.
- Check your bookmarks periodically to remove broken links.
- Use the browser extension for quick saving while browsing.
