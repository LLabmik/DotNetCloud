AI Assistant help

Chatting

Pick a conversation on the left, or press New chat. Messages stream from the model as they are generated; Abort cancels the request, and a request still waiting in the queue gives up its place.

Conversations

Double-click a title to rename it, or use the delete button to remove it. Conversations and their messages are stored in this module's own data store and are deleted when your account is deleted.

Models and providers

The provider, model and endpoint are configured by your administrator. The model shown above the list is the one new conversations use; it cannot be changed from here.

Privacy

Your prompts and the model's answers are sent to the configured language-model provider. This assistant does not read your files, mail, calendar or contacts.
